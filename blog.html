Concept-Based Mechanistic Interpretability Needs a Concrete Evaluation Paradigm

A walkthrough of our NeurIPS 2026 position paper. Reconstruction loss, automated interpretability scores, and the other standard metrics for sparse dictionary learning do not directly measure whether the ground-truth concepts are recovered. We show this with theory and experiments, propose four principles for a concrete evaluation paradigm, and sketch a capsule-based benchmark for concept localization.
